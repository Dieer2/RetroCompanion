Gold is the slow part of Dragon Warrior. The best sword and shield together cost 24,600, and a Slime is listed at two. Knowing where things are sold, and what you can skip, saves hours. All prices are from the NES version, Dragon Warrior (USA).

Erdrick's Armor is not sold either. It is buried in Hauksness and adds 28 to your defense.

Magic Armor protects no better than Full Plate. What the extra 4,700 gold buys is this: it gives back one hit point on every fourth step, and an enemy's HURT and HURTMORE do two thirds of their damage. Fire breath is not weakened by it.

You can carry six Herbs at most. An Herb restores more than the HEAL spell, which gives 10 to 17 hit points.

A shop buys anything back for half of its price.

## Magic Keys

A key is used up when it opens a door, and you can carry six. Three places sell them, at three prices.

| Place | Price for one | Price for six |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rimuldar, the northwest hut | 53 | 318 |
| Tantegel Castle, the back area | 85 | 510 |
| Cantlin, next to the Flame Sword shop | 98 | 588 |

Rimuldar is the cheapest, and it is the only one you can reach without already having a key. To get to the hut, walk around the outside of the moat.

## What to buy, in order

Start with a Club, Clothes and a Dragon's Scale. That is 100 of your 120 gold. Around level 5, replace them with a Copper Sword, Leather Armor and a Small Shield for 340.

In Garinham, buy the Hand Axe first and a Large Shield second. Chain Mail is the one to skip if you are short, because Half Plate costs 700 more and protects better. Buy the Half Plate in Kol or Garinham at about level 10.

In Rimuldar the six keys come first, then a Broad Sword and Full Plate. After that, save everything for the Flame Sword and the Silver Shield in Cantlin. Do not buy Magic Armor on the way. Erdrick's Armor is free and better.

## Two cursed items worth selling

The Death Necklace from the Mountain Cave and the Cursed Belt from Garin's Grave both curse you if you put them on. The necklace sells for 1,200 gold and the belt for 180. If you do get cursed, a man in Brecconary lifts it.

While you are cursed, a game you load starts you with 1 hit point.

The Fighter's Ring from the Mountain Cave can be worn, but it changes nothing in a fight. Putting it on sets a flag that only one townsman's line ever looks at. A shop pays 15 gold for it.
