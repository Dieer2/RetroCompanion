title: Dragon Warrior walkthrough: the whole game in 32 steps
description: A short route through Dragon Warrior on the NES, from the throne room to the Dragonlord, with the level to reach before each part.
date: 2026-11-13

This is the route Retro Companion follows in its overlay. It has 32 steps in eight parts. Prices and places are for the NES version, Dragon Warrior (USA). Distances are counted in tiles from Tantegel Castle, the way the game itself counts them once you hold Gwaelin's Love.

::image overlay

## 1. Setting out

1. In the throne room, take all three chests: 120 gold, a Torch and a Magic Key. The key opens the door and is used up. You can only save by talking to the king.
2. Walk east to Brecconary. Buy a Club (60) and Clothes (20), and a Dragon's Scale (20) in the item shop. Use the scale from the item menu to wear it.
3. Fight Slimes, Red Slimes and Drakees near the castle until level 3. Do not cross a bridge yet. Monsters get stronger past every bridge. At level 3 you learn HEAL, and from then on the old man right of the castle entrance refills your magic for free.
4. Optional: Erdrick's Cave, north and a little west of Tantegel. There are no monsters inside. Light a Torch, go down one floor and read the tablet. It is story only.
5. Earn gold nearby and buy a Copper Sword (180), Leather Armor (70) and a Small Shield (90). Level 4 gives you HURT.

## 2. The north

6. At level 5, find Garinham, far to the northwest. Do not cross the western bridge on the way.
7. In Garinham, buy a Hand Axe (560), Chain Mail (300) and a Large Shield (800). If gold is short, skip the Chain Mail and save for Half Plate.
8. At level 7 you learn SLEEP, which works well on Scorpions and Skeletons. Cross the bridge northeast of Tantegel, then a second one further east. Kol is north of it.
9. In Kol, stand four tiles south of the bath and use SEARCH. You find the Fairy Flute. It needs no key.
10. Train around Kol to level 10 and buy Half Plate (1000). Level 9 gives RADIANT for caves, level 10 gives STOPSPELL.

## 3. Rimuldar

11. South of Kol is a poison swamp that hurts on every step. Enter the Swamp Cave, light it, and walk to the south exit. A side branch holds a Green Dragon. Leave it for later.
12. Keep going south to Rimuldar. The monsters here are stronger than you. Use STOPSPELL on Warlocks, SLEEP on Wolves.
13. The key shop is the northwest hut. You reach it by walking around the outside of the moat. Keys cost 53 each, and you can carry six. Buy six.
14. Train near Rimuldar to level 13. Level 12 gives OUTSIDE, level 13 gives RETURN. Buy a Broad Sword (1500) and Full Plate (3000).

## 4. The three relics

15. Cast RETURN. In Tantegel, open the locked door to the back area. Walk down the east side of the castle to the water, step one tile right, and go around the water to the stairs in the southeast corner. The cellar chest holds the Stones of Sunlight.
16. Bring two keys to Garinham. The large north building needs the first. Inside, walk through the dark wall. The stairs behind the building lead to Garin's Grave. On the first floor a locked door in the bottom right needs the second key. The Silver Harp is on the third floor, reached through the tiny fourth one. Leave with OUTSIDE.
17. The Northern Shrine is west of Kol at the top edge of the map. Walk north of the mountain range to reach it. The old man trades the Silver Harp for the Staff of Rain.

## 5. The south

18. Train to level 15 and save gold. The goal is a Flame Sword (9800) and a Silver Shield (14800). Goldmen pay well, Metal Slimes give the most experience.
19. Go to Cantlin: south from Garinham, west over the bridge below the Mountain Cave, south past the desert, then east. A Golem guards the gate. In the fight, use the Fairy Flute to put it to sleep, then attack.
20. In Cantlin the best shop is behind the locked door in the northeast. Buy the Flame Sword and the Silver Shield.
21. Train to level 17 for HEALMORE. The next two fights are risky without it.
22. In the ruins of Hauksness, on the east side, step on the tree tile behind the swamp-covered shop. An Axe Knight attacks and you cannot run. Cast STOPSPELL until it works, then fight and heal. After you win, stay on the tile and use SEARCH. You find Erdrick's Armor.

## 6. The princess

23. Take one key to the Swamp Cave. Beat the Green Dragon in the side branch, open the door behind it and carry Gwaelin out.
24. Cast OUTSIDE, then RETURN, and bring her to the king. She gives you Gwaelin's Love, which tells your distance from Tantegel.

## 7. The rainbow bridge

25. Erdrick's Token lies in the big swamp southeast of Cantlin, 70 tiles south and 40 tiles east of Tantegel. Use SEARCH on that exact tile.
26. The Southern Shrine is at the southeast tip of the island below Rimuldar. With the Token, the Stones of Sunlight and the Staff of Rain you get the Rainbow Drop.
27. Walk north of Rimuldar to the shore and west along it to the tip of the peninsula, where one tile of water is left between you and the island. Use the Rainbow Drop there.

## 8. Charlock

28. Train to level 19 or 20. Level 19 gives HURTMORE.
29. In Charlock Castle the two staircases you can see are false paths. Stand behind the throne and use SEARCH. A hidden staircase opens.
30. Erdrick's Sword is in a closed room on the second basement floor. You reach it from the fourth floor, by taking the stairs that lead back up. Then leave, save and rest.
31. The Dragonlord waits on the bottom floor. Answer NO to his offer. His first form is weak. Against the dragon, only attack and cast HEALMORE. Heal when you drop below about 50 hit points.
32. Return to Tantegel and talk to the king.
