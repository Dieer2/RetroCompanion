    Questions

    How do I get the supporter mark?

    Write your Retro Companion player name in the message when you pay. I add the mark by hand, usually within two days. You need a free account in the app for it.

    What is Ko-fi?

    A payment page for small projects. You pay there by card or PayPal. This site never sees your card details.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes, any time, on Ko-fi. The mark and your place on the list stay until the paid month ends.

    Does a supporter get games earlier?

    No. A new game goes out to everyone on the same day.

    I cannot pay. Can I still help?

    Yes. Tell another player about the program, or report a walkthrough step that was wrong or unclear. Both help a lot.
