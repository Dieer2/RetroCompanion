What's new

New things in the program and on this site, newest first. The program shows the same list in its library window.

14 November 2026

Version 0.8

  • The program updates itself. It looks for a new version when it starts, tells you at the bottom of the library when there is one, and "Update and restart" downloads it, checks it against the checksum of the release and starts the new version. Your settings and progress stay as they are. The check can be switched off in the settings

14 November 2026

Version 0.7

  • Ideas: suggest a feature and vote on the ideas of other players, in the program (Ideas, at the bottom of the library) and on the website. The list shows what is planned, what is open for votes and what is done. It needs a free account; one account has one vote on each idea

14 November 2026

Version 0.6

  • The shop has more to spend points on: three more achievement chimes (Coin, Bells, Harp; each can be heard before buying), two more pop-up styles (a slim banner, and a card edged in the grade's colour) and bronze, silver, gold and platinum frames for the profile picture
  • An earned achievement can also show as a message inside RetroArch, which helps in exclusive fullscreen where the overlay cannot show. RetroArch can save a screenshot at that moment too. Both are in the settings
  • Discord can show the game you are playing, your level and place, and the step you are on. It is off until you switch it on in the settings

14 November 2026

Version 0.5

  • Achievement pop-up: an earned achievement slides in at the top of the screen with the trophy of its grade and Retro Companion's own chime. The sound can be switched off in the overlay's right-click menu
  • Earned achievements carry a trophy in the colour of their grade in the overlay's achievements window
  • Full-size map (F, or the corner icon of the map window): the whole of Alefgard with place names. Drag it to move, turn the wheel to zoom, click a place to read what is there
  • The map tells for every town, cave and shrine whether you are done there, still have something to do, will need it later in the walkthrough, or never need it at all
  • Every town, cave and dungeon floor has its own map with the chests, what is in them, and where each staircase leads. Filters switch towns, caves, quest spots, chests, stairs, monster zones, the route and the names on and off
  • Profile picture in the library: twenty built-in portraits, or your own PNG or GIF picture
  • This list: what is new, in the program and on the website

14 November 2026

Version 0.4

  • Compact form for every overlay window: one strip with an icon and the main figures, so the game stays visible. C switches all of them (Ctrl+Shift+C in RetroArch)

14 November 2026

Version 0.3

  • A beaten enemy now reads 0 HP in the battle window instead of a full bar
  • Achievements window in the overlay: every achievement of the game, one group at a time
  • Event log window: enemies beaten, levels, items, finished steps and achievements, newest first

14 November 2026

Open source

  • Retro Companion is open source under the MIT license. The program is downloaded from its release page on GitHub, where it is built from the public source

13 November 2026

Guides

  • Six Dragon Warrior guides on the website: the 32-step walkthrough, hidden items, levels and spells, shop prices, facts read from the game's code, and the game's history
  • Tools inside the guides: experience, damage and gold calculators, Gwaelin's Love, tickable steps

13 November 2026

Supporters

  • A supporter page on the website. Retro Companion stays free; supporters pay for the server and the time each new game takes

12 November 2026

Version 0.2
