Privacy

The app works without an account, and then nothing leaves your computer. This page is about the optional account. It keeps your trophies when you play on another computer and puts you on the leaderboard.

What the server keeps

  • Your email address and player name
  • Your password as a scrypt hash. The password itself is not stored
  • Sign-in tokens, also as hashes, so the app stays signed in
  • Game data from the app: achievements with their dates, achievement counters, walkthrough steps marked done, play time, the last place seen and the shop items you bought

Your settings stay on your computer. That includes where your ROMs are and your MobyGames key.

What is public

The leaderboard shows your player name, points, level and trophy counts. It never shows your email address.

Ideas and votes

On the ideas page and in the app you can suggest a feature and vote. The server keeps the idea with your account, and which ideas you voted for. An idea is public once I have read it, with your player name next to it; votes show only as a number. When you sign in on the website, the sign-in token is kept in your browser until you sign out. Deleting the account removes your votes and your unpublished ideas; published ideas stay without your name.

Supporters

Payments happen on Ko-fi, under Ko-fi's own privacy policy. This server never sees your card details. If you support the project and tell me your player name, the server keeps your supporter tier with your account. Your player name and tier then show on the supporters list and the leaderboard. Write to me if you support the project but want to stay off the list.

Addresses and logs

The web server keeps no access log. To slow down password guessing, the server counts sign-in attempts per network address in memory for up to an hour. These counts are not saved anywhere.

Visit counting

The site counts page views, downloads and the site a visitor came from, on this server only. It uses no cookie and no outside analytics service. Your network address is not stored. To tell a returning visitor from a new one on the same day, the server keeps a one-way fingerprint made from your address and browser name with a key that is replaced every day. The fingerprints are deleted when the day ends and never go into a backup. What stays is plain numbers per day.

Updates

When it starts, the app asks GitHub whether there is a newer version, and downloads it from there if you choose to update. GitHub sees your network address for that request, as with any download; nothing about you or your games is sent. You can switch the check off in the app's settings.

Where

The server is a Hetzner machine in Helsinki, Finland. Password reset emails go out through Resend, which sees your email address for that one message.

How long

Your data stays until you delete the account. In the app, open Profile and click Delete account. The account and its data are removed from the server at once. Database backups roll over within 14 days, and Hetzner's own server backups within 7.

Your rights

You can see your data in the app and delete it there. Your own copy is in the settings file on your computer.
